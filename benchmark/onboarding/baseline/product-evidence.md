## User feedback

Recent anonymised comments:

- “Why do you need my industry before I can even see the product?”
- “I just wanted to try it.”
- “I don't know what you mean by workspace.”
- “I wasn't sure if company size was asking about my team or the whole company.”
- “There were too many questions before I'd seen anything useful.”
- “I'm a freelancer, none of this really felt like it was for me.”

## Product constraints

- A workspace name must be persisted before onboarding completes.
- Company size and industry are useful for later personalisation but are not required to create a workspace.
- Marketing consent must remain optional and must not be bundled into completion.
- Do not introduce a backend or external service for this benchmark.
- Preserve the existing Northstar visual language rather than redesigning the entire product.
